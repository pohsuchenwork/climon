## What is here

| File | Purpose |

## Placeholders you must fill before use

Search the documents for `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` and complete each:

- Operator legal name and entity type (individual, LLC, Ltd, etc.). Use the correct legal
  name of whoever operates the service.
- Contact email (use a dedicated address you are willing to publish) and, where required, a
  mailing address.
- Governing law and venue (use a jurisdiction you actually have a connection to).
- Minimum age.
- Liability cap amount (a nominal figure, for example USD 50).
- Hosting region and provider (currently a Render-hosted server).
- Effective and last-updated dates.

## Highest-priority items for the lawyer (see the closing note in each document)

   `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` Section 9.)

   and venue clauses for consumers. (`TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` Sections 10-12, 15.)
3. **Privacy law (GDPR/CCPA) and children** - lawful basis, data-subject rights, transfers, a
   possible data processing agreement with the host, and a children's-data position.
   (`PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.)
4. **Acceptance mechanism** - browsewrap by use is weaker than an affirmative click; consider
   an in-product acceptance step for online play. (`TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` Section 1.)
