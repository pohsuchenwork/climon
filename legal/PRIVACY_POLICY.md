personal data as possible.

## 2. Data we process

**(a) Display name (online play).** When you play online, you choose a display name. We
transmit that name to the one opponent you are matched with, so each of you can see who you
are playing. The name is processed in memory only for the duration of your session and is
**not stored in a database**; our server keeps no persistent record of it. Please do not put
your real name, contact details, or other sensitive information in your display name, as it
is shown to your opponent.

**(b) Connection data.** To route your online connection, our server and our hosting and
network providers necessarily process technical connection data, which includes your IP
address and similar metadata. Our application identifies each connection with a randomly
generated session token (not derived from your identity) and logs only basic connection
events (connect and disconnect) for operational purposes; **it does not store your IP address
or display name in its own application logs.** Our hosting and network providers may process
or log connection metadata under their own policies. [FLAG: confirm what your hosting
provider, currently [PLACEHOLDER: hosting provider, for example Render], logs and for how
long, and whether you need a data processing agreement with them.]

**(c) Data stored locally on your own device.** CLImon stores a configuration file and a log
file **on your own computer** (by default under your user config and state directories). These
may contain the server address you connect to, your chosen display name, your theme and
motion settings, and local diagnostic log lines. These files stay on your device and are not
transmitted to us. You control them and can delete them.

We do not knowingly collect any other personal data, and we do not process special-category
(sensitive) data.

## 3. Why we process it and our legal basis

We process the limited data above only to provide the online play you request (to match you
with an opponent and run the battle). Where the GDPR or similar law applies, our legal basis
is performance of the service you ask for and our legitimate interest in operating and
securing the Service. [FLAG: a lawyer should confirm the correct lawful basis and whether any

## 4. Retention

Connection and session data are processed transiently and are not retained by our application
after your session ends. Operational logs, if any, are minimal and short-lived. Data your
hosting and network providers process is retained under their policies. Local files on your
device persist until you delete them.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure

We share data only as follows: (a) your display name is shown to the single opponent you are
matched with; (b) our hosting and network providers process connection data as our service
providers/processors to operate the Service; and (c) we may disclose data if required by law
or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the Service. **We do not sell or share your
personal data for advertising or marketing, and we do not use it to build profiles.** For the
purposes of US state privacy laws, we do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those
terms are defined.
